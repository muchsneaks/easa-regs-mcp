3. No cookies, no tracking, no external content

This website uses no cookies, no analytics or marketing tools, no social media plugins and no embedded videos. Fonts and images are loaded from the same server as the website itself; nothing is loaded from Google Fonts or other third parties.

4. Language selection

On your first visit the website reads the language set in your browser to show you the German or English version. This happens only inside your browser; the information is neither transmitted nor stored.

If you actively switch the language, the website stores your choice ("de" or "en") in your browser's local storage (localStorage) so it is kept on your next visit. This entry never leaves your device and is not sent to me. Storing it is strictly necessary for the service you explicitly requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG); the legal basis for processing is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You can delete the entry at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

5. Hosted connector (remote MCP server)

If you add easa-regs as a connector in Claude or another AI application (MCP client), the application calls the easa-regs tools when needed. The following is sent to the server:

  • the content of the tool call, i.e. the search query or rule reference the AI derives from your question (e.g. "SEP revalidation" or "FCL.740.A") and optional filters;
  • technical connection data, in particular the IP address of the calling system and the time of the request.

With Claude.ai and the Claude apps, calls are usually made from Anthropic's servers, so the server normally does not see your own IP address. With locally running applications (e.g. Claude Code, Cursor) the request is sent directly from your device.

The server answers the request from the public EASA rule index and then forgets it. It is stateless, creates no user accounts, receives no chat history and logs neither requests nor IP addresses. Still, please avoid putting unnecessary personal data into questions answered via the connector, as the AI may pass parts of your question on as a search term.

Hosting: The server runs at Render Services, Inc., San Francisco, USA, in the Frankfurt am Main data centre region. Render processes the connection data as a processor under a contract pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR (Data Processing Addendum) and may log it briefly to operate and secure its platform. Render Services, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). More information: Render Privacy Policy.

Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is delivering the rule texts you requested and operating the service securely.

The provider of the AI application itself (e.g. Anthropic for Claude) is solely responsible for how it processes your conversations, under its own privacy policy; for Anthropic, see the Anthropic Privacy Policy.

6. Claude Desktop extension and local installation

The Claude Desktop extension and the local installation run entirely on your computer; your queries are not sent to easa-regs. On first start and at most once a week afterwards, the program downloads the public rule index from GitHub. GitHub, Inc. technically receives your IP address and the usual connection data. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (keeping the rule texts current); for third-country transfers see section 2.

7. GitHub repository and downloads

Source code, releases and the extension file are provided on GitHub. If you follow links to GitHub or download files there, GitHub, Inc. processes your data as an independent controller under the GitHub privacy statement linked above.

8. Links to other websites

This website links to external sites, e.g. EASA, Anthropic, Cursor, arXiv and Stanford University. They are only loaded when you click a link; from then on the respective provider's privacy policy applies.

9. Contact by email

If you email me, I process your email address, your name (if given) and the content of your message to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry concerns a contract or pre-contractual steps, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries). I delete the data once your enquiry has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Emails are stored with my email provider, acting as a processor.

10. Retention

I store personal data only as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as required by statutory retention periods. The connector server stores no requests. Hosting providers delete their server log files automatically according to their own retention periods.
